How to read this map Open guide

Reading rule

Follow the records, not the visual distance.

PMM is an evidence-aware knowledge map. Nearby nodes help navigation only; they do not show a stronger effect, a causal direction, or a scientific consensus.

Shapes

Object cardA construct, mechanism, state, behavior, intervention, measurement, context, event, outcome, contingency, or observation.

Mechanism pillA proposed or established process with specified roles and conditions. It is not automatically proven by appearing on the map.

Claim cardA scoped scientific assertion. Open it to see its status, confidence, evidence, limitations, and sources.

Research questionA faint dashed card marks an important gap suggested by mapped limitations. It is a question, not a finding or evidence rating.

Claim colours

Green: supportedThe mapped evidence supports the stated claim within its declared scope.

Amber: mixed or unsupportedResults disagree, are null, or do not currently support the claim.

Blue: proposedA falsifiable mechanism or causal hypothesis, not an established result.

Lines and interaction

Solid lineA structural or operational relation, such as “measured by” or “occurs in context.”

Dashed arrowConnects an object to a Claim. The Claim, not the arrow, carries the empirical or causal interpretation.

Cross-family index

One inventory, without false unification.

Each card is a Mechanism record from one evidence pack. Counts show traceability to modeled Claims, Evidence records, and Sources. They are not truth scores, effect sizes, or scientific rankings.

Similar labels do not establish that mechanisms are identical or causally connected. Cross-family bridges require separate scientific review.
